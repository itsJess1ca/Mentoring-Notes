## Salary Questions
- **Avoid giving away current salary**, this is a common way to potentially limit your potential. There is no requirement for you to provide this information, and recruiters will tend to use it to put you in a similar band. 
	- If asked your current salary, you are well within your rights to say you're not comfortable, or don't want to, share that information
- When asked **"What's your expected salary"**, again don't provide numbers. Something along the lines of "I've been seeing varied salaries while searching roles, so don't really have a solid number. I'd love to hear what the potential salary is with you at {company}?"

## Coding Challenge Interviews
- I've compiled a [mock interview challenges repo](https://github.com/itsJess1ca/Mock-Interview-Challenges) with a series of challenges for testing some interview style questions.
	- If doing these solo, give yourself an hour and see how far you get. 
	- ### **You're rarely expected to finish** during a coding interview
	- It's more about seeing how you work through a problem. If you have a mentor or someone you can review this with, record yourself doing this to review later. 
	- Try and talk out loud about your thought process and what you're doing whenever doing this, it's a key thing interviewers will rely on.
