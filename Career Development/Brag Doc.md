A brag document is your log/diary of things you've done in your career that you're proud of. Big or small, it doesn't matter. Learned something new? write it down! Implemented a new feature or fixed a bug? Write it down!
You can consult your brag doc when pushing for promotions, raises, or new jobs. They're also a great thing to look back on when you're dealing with the dreaded imposter syndrome we all get.
